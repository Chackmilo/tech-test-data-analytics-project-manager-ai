# Vendor notice — storefront settlement feed

**From:** Partner Integrations, storefront aggregator
**To:** Twin Hearth Studios — data platform team
**Date:** 21 August 2026
**Subject:** Scheduled platform maintenance and API migration freeze

---

As part of our own platform upgrade, the settlement reporting API will be under a **change freeze
from Monday 5 October 2026 through Monday 19 October 2026 inclusive.**

During the freeze window:

- Existing integrations continue to receive data as normal.
- **No new integrations can be provisioned, and no credentials can be issued or rotated.**
- Migrations from v2 to v3 of the settlement endpoint cannot be started or completed.

Partners planning a v3 migration should complete it before 2 October or schedule it after
20 October. Our onboarding team estimates **9 working days** for a partner of your volume, from
credential issue to verified first full settlement file.

We are unable to make exceptions to the freeze window; it applies to all partners.
